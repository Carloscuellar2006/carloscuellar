Every Big Spring Cleaning call-to-action must state this exact order:

1. The homeowner fills in the form on the page.
2. Big Spring texts them.
3. They reply **to that text** with a photo of what needs cleaning, or just their address.
4. The property is sized from satellite and 3D imagery, and a firm price comes back — no walkthrough, no salesman in the driveway.

They can alternatively call or text (256) 677-5992 directly.

**Why:** Carlos corrected copy that read "Text us a photo or your address — we'll send back a firm price," because it implied the homeowner could text first and skip the form. The form is what captures the lead into GHL; a viewer who texts cold never enters the funnel.

**How to apply:** Any CTA — landing page, video end card, yard sign, door hanger, referral sheet — leads with the form and puts the photo *after* the text. Note the whole promise depends on GHL form `CWwez29HSBXvq9NLhbug` having a phone field and an SMS automation that actually fires on submit; that has never been verified.
