When asked to choose between approaches, Carlos answers "just do what you recommend."
He picked the recommended option on both questions in the Doors build.

**Why:** He is delegating the judgment call, not the decision-making. Surveying
options back at him is work he already asked me to do.

**How to apply:** State the recommendation and build it, noting the assumption in
one line. Reserve questions for forks where a wrong guess wastes real work — e.g.
"connect a real bank via Plaid (needs a backend)" vs "enter the balance by hand"
was worth asking; "which shade of green" is not. See [[doors-app]].
