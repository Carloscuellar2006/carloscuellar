As of Sept 2026 Carlos is targeting a specific Huntsville buyer, not the general market:

- Household income $150,000+, home value $500,000+.
- Largely aerospace/defense engineers — busy, technical, time-poor.
- **Their primary worry is property damage and protecting home value. Price is not the objection.**

What this means for every asset:

- Sell on mechanism and risk removal, not on cheapness or discounts. Explain *how* the work avoids damage (soft wash, pre-treatment instead of force, low pressure on siding) — this audience buys technical credibility.
- Lead with pressure washing, not window cleaning: the damage fear is a pressure-washing fear, and all ten before/after pairs are pressure washing. Window cleaning is the low-friction repeat purchase (2-4x/year) and belongs as the co-service, not the hero.
- Narrow service areas to matching neighborhoods (Hampton Cove, Jones Valley, Ledges, Providence, Monte Sano) rather than the whole metro.

**Why:** Carlos defined this ICP himself; it inverts the usual pressure-washing playbook, which competes on price and discounts.

**How to apply:** The blocker is that he is **not insured** — see [[marketing-claim-constraints]]. For this exact audience an uninsured contractor is disqualifying, it is the first question they ask, and it also blocks the Google Guaranteed badge on Local Services Ads where these buyers search. Insurance is the highest-leverage purchase for this strategy; raise it whenever this ICP comes up.

He also removed the "stays clean 6 months" guarantee (Sept 2026), leaving only the walk-through re-clean — a *quality* promise, while this audience wants a *damage* promise. That gap closes only with insurance.
