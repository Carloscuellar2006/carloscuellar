Big Spring Cleaning Exteriors and Pressure Washing (Huntsville, AL) marketing must respect these, across the website, yard signs, door hangers, video ads, and GHL automations:

- **Prices: jobs are quoted per property, but some dollar figures are now on the home page by Carlos's choice (Sept 21, 2026).** The driveway offer shows struck-through values for walkway / porch & steps / sidewalk ($75 / $65 / $60 → $0) — those three are **placeholders Claude invented**; Carlos has never given real numbers, and "~~$75~~ $0" is a reference-price claim, so keep asking for his real à-la-carte prices. Service plans show $150 / $100 / $50 off per cleaning (Monthly / Quarterly / Biannual) — Carlos confirmed keeping these after being told they came from another company's page.
- **Not insured yet.** Never write "insured" or promise to cover damage. This also blocks a No-Damage guarantee until general liability is in place.
- **"Licensed" is unverified** — Carlos has not confirmed it. Treat it the same as "insured" until he does.
- **Two guarantees exist:** (1) he walks the property with you before he leaves and re-cleans anything you don't like on the spot; (2) the **7-Day Rain Guarantee** — if it rains within 7 days of a window cleaning and leaves spots, he re-cleans free — included on Monthly and Quarterly plans only (confirmed Sept 21, 2026). The "stays clean 6 months" warranty was **removed in Sept 2026** — do not reinstate it, and do not invent stronger ones.
- **Services confirmed:** pressure washing, window cleaning (frames and sills included), hard water removal.
- **Never use another company's trademark.** Carlos brought in a competitor's plan cards naming "RAINSHIELD™"; it was removed. Watch for ™/® names in anything he pastes from other sites.
- **"Google Guaranteed" is claimed on the old home page but he does not have it.** That badge is a real Google certification requiring license and insurance verification plus a background check. Remove it wherever it appears. Same for "Background Checked Crews" — he is a one-person operation.
- **No unverified statistics** — no years in business, homes served, crew counts, or awards. Real Google reviews exist from "Liam" and "Kiman"; reference them only as 5-star Google reviews.
- **Don't use other companies' photos or video.** Carlos asked about sourcing clips online after seeing a competitor do it — that's copyright infringement plus deceptive advertising, since it implies the work is his.
- **Don't text the acquired painted-house lead list** — no consent, TCPA exposure, and it risks his A2P registration.

**Why:** These are legal and consistency limits, not style preferences. Each one has already come up and been decided; re-litigating them wastes his time, and violating one creates real exposure.

**How to apply:** Check any new marketing asset against this list before building it. When a constraint blocks the obvious move (e.g. a competitor ad opens on a price), say so and solve around it rather than quietly copying. See [[big-spring-cta-sequence]].
