01 — Who We Are

We treat every driveway, deck, and storefront like it's the one out front of our own house.

Big Spring Cleaning Exteriors is a locally owned pressure washing and exterior cleaning company serving homeowners and businesses throughout Huntsville, AL and the surrounding area. We show up on time, communicate clearly, and leave every surface looking new — no shortcuts, no upsells, no surprises on the invoice.

We clean with low PSI and high GPM — high volume of water rather than high force — so nothing gets etched, striped or driven behind your siding. Before we rinse, we apply a property protectant chemical that breaks the grime down on its own. Once the chemical has done the work, the pressure never has to.

02 — Services

What We Clean

House Washing Soft wash that kills the algae at its root instead of shearing it off the surface. Safe on siding, brick and painted trim. Driveways & Walkways Red clay, oil and traffic film pulled out of the concrete, not smeared around in it. Walkway and front porch included free. Window Cleaning Inside and out, with the frames and sills included. Screens washed and tracks wiped, so the whole opening is clean — not just the glass. Roof & Gutter Cleaning Black streaks treated and rinsed, never pressured, so the shingle granules stay put. Includes gutter whitening — the faces come back to their original color. Paver Cleaning Cleaned without stripping the sealant or blowing the joint sand out of the joints. We can re-sand and re-seal while we’re there if you want it. Red Clay Stain Removal Alabama red clay and rust go deep into the pores of concrete. We draw them out chemically rather than trying to blast them off. Decks & Fences Weathered wood and vinyl brought back to color at low pressure, so the grain doesn’t fuzz up and the boards don’t furr. Patios & Pool Decking Pavers, stamped concrete and natural stone. Cleaned so the surface isn’t slick underfoot the next time it rains.

Also servicing: garage floors · sidewalks · retaining walls · brick & masonry · storefronts

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we quote per job. Fill in the form and we’ll text you — reply with a photo or your address and you’ll get a firm price back, usually the same day.

Will it damage my concrete, siding or plants?

No. We clean with low PSI and high GPM — lots of water, not lots of force — and apply a protectant first so the grime breaks down on its own. Plants get rinsed before and after.

What comes free with a driveway?

Your front walkway, front porch and steps, and sidewalk — cleaned at no extra charge every time we do a driveway.

Do you clean windows?

Yes — inside and out, with the frames and sills included. We also remove hard water spots.

What’s the 7-Day Rain Guarantee?

If it rains within 7 days of your window cleaning and leaves spots, we come back and re-clean them for free. It’s included on our Monthly and Quarterly plans.

How do the window cleaning plans work?

The plans are for window cleaning. Pick how often you want us out — monthly, quarterly or twice a year — and you save on every cleaning: $150 a visit on Monthly, $100 on Quarterly, $50 on Biannual. Every plan also takes a percentage off everything else we clean — 40% on Monthly, 25% on Quarterly, 15% on Biannual. Monthly and Quarterly add the 7-Day Rain Guarantee and free hard water removal.

Do I need to be home?

No. Just move the cars off the driveway. If you’re home when we finish, we’ll walk it with you — if you don’t like something, we re-clean it on the spot.

What areas do you serve?

Huntsville and the surrounding areas.
